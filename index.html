    🏆 Smart India Hackathon Pitch & Engineering Summary

    Consolidated presentation deck outline and key engineering answers for jury evaluation.

    1. Sense → Analyze → Predict → Act

    Conventional irrigation systems only look at the present soil condition. Our innovation bridges **on-field IoT sensors with AI weather forecasting**. If soil moisture is low but heavy rain is forecasted in the next few hours, the system delays irrigation, saving electricity, preserving reservoir water, and preventing nutrient leaching.

    2. Protecting Nutrients & Water Quality

    Over-irrigation flushes dissolved nitrates ($NO_3^-$) and pesticide residues out of the active root zone into the groundwater table. By delivering water only when and where required, the prototype prevents fertilizer loss, lowers farmer input costs, and protects local water resources.

    3. 100% Offline Edge Resilience

    Rural internet failure does not compromise irrigation safety. The ESP32 executes the core closed-loop sense-and-control logic fully on-device. Weather data and model weight updates sync asynchronously whenever connectivity is restored.

    4. High Return on Investment (ROI)

    At a component target cost under **₹2,000 per field unit**, the system pays for itself in less than one harvest season through a **30-40% reduction in water pumping electricity** and a **25% reduction in wasted fertilizer applications**.
